WHAT IS MMC

MORE THAN A CLUB

MMC — MSU Music Club is the official music society of Management & Science University (MSU), founded to nurture musical talent, foster creativity, and build a vibrant community around the love of music.

Whether you play guitar, sing, produce beats, or simply appreciate great music, MMC is your home. We run workshops, open mic nights, recording sessions, and our annual flagship concert — all student-led and student-driven.

We believe music has the power to connect, heal, and inspire. Our door is open to every MSU student regardless of skill level — beginners and seasoned performers alike are welcomed with equal enthusiasm.
