Fuzz Who we are

Not just a club. A community built around sound.

Music is more than something we listen to. It's something we create, perform, experience, and share.

MSU Music Club is a student-led community at Management & Science University that brings together people with a shared passion for music. Whether you're a vocalist, guitarist, drummer, producer, songwriter, instrumentalist, or simply someone who loves being surrounded by music, MMC is a place to connect.

We believe music should be explored, not restricted. It doesn't matter whether you're stepping onto a stage for the first time or you've been performing for years. What matters is the passion you bring.

Our philosophy

  1. Express. Find your voice, discover your style, and make music that represents you.
  2. Connect. Meet people who share your passion. Create friendships, collaborations, and memories through music.
  3. Evolve. Keep learning, experimenting, practising, and discovering what you're capable of.
  4. Perform. Turn preparation into performance and share your creativity with others.

Different people. Different sounds. One community.

Chorus Every sound belongs

What's your frequency?

There is no single way to experience music. Some people live for the stage. Others prefer the studio, the rehearsal room, or the creative process behind a performance. Wherever your interests lie, there's a place for your passion to grow.

Six voices, one chorus. These are areas of interest, not formal club divisions.

01 / 06 · The voice

Vocals

Every voice carries a story. Explore your vocal identity, develop confidence, and discover the possibilities of musical expression.

02 / 06 · The strings

Guitar & bass

From subtle melodies to powerful riffs, strings bring character, energy, and emotion to music. Explore rhythm, melody, and the art of playing together.

03 / 06 · The rhythm

Drums & percussion

The heartbeat behind the music. Discover the power of timing, groove, coordination, and musical energy.

04 / 06 · The melody

Keys & instruments

Every chord can change the feeling of a song. Explore harmony, musical arrangements, and the possibilities of instrumental expression.

05 / 06 · The creation

Songwriting & production

Every song begins somewhere. Turn ideas into lyrics, melodies, arrangements, and original musical concepts.

06 / 06 · The stage

Live performance

A performance is more than playing the right notes. It's about presence, confidence, communication, and creating a moment people remember.

Echo What happens at MMC

The music doesn't stop when the song ends.

Music is built through experiences. The rehearsals. The mistakes. The breakthroughs. The first performances. The people you meet along the way. Explore the kinds of musical activities and opportunities associated with the MMC community.

  1. 01

    Live performances

    The stage is yours.

    Music is meant to be experienced. Live performances bring musicians and audiences together, turning hours of practice into shared moments.

  2. 02

    Open mic sessions

    One mic. Endless possibilities.

    Sometimes all you need is the courage to start. Open mic sessions give musicians a welcoming setting to express themselves, experiment, and build confidence.

  3. 03

    Music workshops

    Learn it. Play it. Own it.

    Musical growth never stops. Workshops create chances to explore techniques, exchange knowledge, and learn alongside fellow music enthusiasts.

  4. 04

    Rehearsals & jams

    Find the groove.

    Great music often begins with musicians playing together. Rehearsals and jam sessions encourage experimentation, collaboration, and new ideas.

  5. 05

    Recording & creation

    From an idea to a sound.

    A melody, a lyric, or a simple rhythm can become something bigger. Explore the process of developing, arranging, and recording music.

  6. 06

    Community & collaboration

    Good music. Better connections.

    The best part of music is often the people you share it with. MMC brings together students with different influences, talents, and experiences.

Looper The creative process

Before the applause. There's the work.

The final performance is only part of the story. Behind every musical moment are ideas, preparation, teamwork, and hours spent bringing everything together.

Layer 1 · Record

01 The idea

It starts with inspiration. A melody, a rhythm, a conversation, or the desire to create something different.

Layer 2 · Overdub

02 The practice

Ideas become stronger through repetition. Musicians experiment, refine their techniques, and learn to work together.

Layer 3 · Overdub

03 The production

A performance involves more than the people on stage. Equipment, technical coordination, logistics, media, and event organisation all contribute to the experience.

Layer 4 · Play

04 The moment

The lights come on. The music begins. And everything that happened behind the scenes becomes part of the performance.
